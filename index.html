01 · About

Privacy-preserving proofs, from protocol to product

I'm a software engineer focused on zkTLS, the family of techniques (MPC-TLS, proxy witnesses and TEEs) that turns an ordinary HTTPS session into a verifiable proof. Today I build zkTLS solutions at Burnt Labs. Before that I worked with Reclaim Protocol, where I built the SDKs and developer tools that cut integration time from hours to minutes.

I like working across the whole stack: protocol research and Rust verifiers, mobile SDKs, and the developer experience that makes all of it usable. My M.Tech at IIIT Hyderabad was in Computer Science & Information Security, and I have shipped work in NLP, information retrieval and database internals.
